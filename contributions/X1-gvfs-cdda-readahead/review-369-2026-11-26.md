## What each asks (to be worked out in the revision)

- **1:** treat a `first_track` of `CDIO_INVALID_TRACK` as an error too.
- **2:** one validation helper used by `do_open_for_read()` and `do_query_info()`.
- **3:** the error message names libcdio, not paranoia. A new translatable string; the old one is used elsewhere.
- **4:** either support discs whose first track is not 1 everywhere (`do_enumerate()`, `set_info_for_track()`), or
  keep the assumption that tracks start at 1 and drop `first_track` from the check.
  - This overlaps X3 in `docs/issues.md`. Which way is right needs the libcdio and CD-standard facts first.
