cdda: Read ahead so that audio playback does not stall

When paranoia's cache is used up, cdio_paranoia_read() reads a whole
cache model of sectors (1200 by default, 16 s of audio) before it
returns. do_read() calls it synchronously for each client read, so
every 16 s one read waits while the drive reads the next 16 s: about
3 s on a USB drive that has spun down in the meantime. A player that
pulls the track through GIO, such as Totem, runs dry, and the audio
stops for about 3 s every 16 s.

Read ahead in a worker thread that keeps up to 10 s of the sectors
following the one the client wants in a ring, and serve do_read() from
it. Only the worker calls into paranoia once it has started; a seek
outside the ring restarts it there, and a read error is reported to
the client as before.
