cdda: Limit the size of audio read refills

Playing an audio CD track through gvfs, for example in Totem, stops
for about 3 seconds every 16 seconds on a USB drive. do_read() gets one
sector at a time from cdio_paranoia_read(), but whenever paranoia's
buffer is used up, that call first reads a whole cache model from the
drive: 1200 sectors by default, 16 seconds of audio, also in the
PARANOIA_MODE_DISABLE used here. The client's read waits until all of
it has arrived, and a player reading the track in real time runs dry.

Set the cache model to 150 sectors, 2 seconds of audio, as GStreamer's
cdparanoiasrc does for playback. Each refill then takes a fraction of
a second. The sectors read and the data returned are the same.

Tested with an ASUS SDRW-08D2S-U USB drive and Totem reading through
gvfsd-fuse, playing a 5:09 track twice per build from a stopped drive:
without this change the player's stream ran dry 19 times in the middle
of the track in both runs, with it not once. Copying the whole track
took 29.8 s before and 29.0 s after, and the audio was identical to a
direct read with cd-paranoia.

Builds against a libcdio-paranoia without cdio_paranoia_cachemodel_size()
keep the current behaviour.
