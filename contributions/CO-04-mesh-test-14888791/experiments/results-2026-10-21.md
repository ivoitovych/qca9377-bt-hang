## Result: no Tested-by
**It introduces a circular-locking report.** It takes `hdev->lock` in
`mesh_send_start_complete()` (and, in the widened-window runs, `mesh_next()`) while
`hci_cmd_sync_clear()` holds `cmd_sync_work_lock`, the reverse of the existing order.

| set | base | + CO-05 only | 14888791 alone | 14888791 + CO-05 |

Reached without any scratch delay, in the `-s power` cases. **[inference]** It could deadlock if
another path holds `hdev->lock` and queues a sync entry while the clear runs.
