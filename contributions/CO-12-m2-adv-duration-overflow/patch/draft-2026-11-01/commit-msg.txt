Bluetooth: hci_sync: Fix extended advertising duration overflow

hci_enable_ext_advertising_sync() computes the advertising set Duration
from the instance timeout in a u16. The timeout comes from MGMT Add
Advertising or Add Extended Advertising Parameters in seconds, so any
timeout above 65 s wraps: 66 s is sent as a Duration of 46 (460 ms) and
the instance is removed after less than half a second, 655 s becomes
65.17 s, and timeouts congruent to 0 or 6357 modulo 8192 encode
Duration 0, which the controller reads as no limit, so such an instance
is never removed.

Compute the duration in a u32 and limit the encoded value to the
field's maximum of 0xffff (655.35 s). Timeouts up to 655 s are now
passed exactly; longer ones end at 655.35 s instead of at an arbitrary
earlier time or never.
