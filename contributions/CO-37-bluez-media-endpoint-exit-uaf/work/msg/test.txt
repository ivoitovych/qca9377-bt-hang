test: Cover A2DP endpoint release with an acquired transport

Add a host plugin registering an A2DP Source endpoint through
RegisterApplication, as sound servers do, and acquiring its transport,
and two tests releasing the endpoint while the stream is locked: when
the application leaves the bus, and when bluetoothd loses the bus and
exits without powering the adapter off.

Built with AddressSanitizer, bluetoothd reports a heap-use-after-free
in media_endpoint_remove() in both without the first patch, and in
clear_endpoint() on exit without the second.
