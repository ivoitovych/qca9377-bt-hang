PROPOSAL, local experiment: a2dp: unregister a removed SEP from idle on unlock

Not part of the series. A SEP removed while one of its streams was
locked is unregistered by a2dp_sep_unlock(), which the transport code
calls while it still uses the stream, its setup and the transport: the
suspend completion of a Release, or media_transport_free() while the
endpoint's transports are cleared. Unregistering calls the endpoint's
destroy callback, which releases the endpoint and destroys those
transports, or frees the endpoint, under the callers.

Unregister such a SEP from a timeout instead, cancelled if the SEP is
unregistered first; if a stream was locked again meanwhile, the next
unlock unregisters it. SEPs still waiting when the server goes are
dropped.
