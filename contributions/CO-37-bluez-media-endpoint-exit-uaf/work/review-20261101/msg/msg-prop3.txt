PROPOSAL, local experiment: media: release a deferred A2DP endpoint from idle

Not part of the series. A removal of an A2DP endpoint deferred while
another client's transport locked the stream completes in
a2dp_sep_unlock(), called from the code of a transport of that endpoint
(the suspend completion of a Release, or media_transport_free() while
release_endpoint() clears the transports). Releasing the endpoint there
destroys that transport, or frees the endpoint, under its callers.

Release such an endpoint from an idle callback instead, cancelled if
the endpoint is freed first; a SEP unregistered directly by
media_endpoint_remove() is released at once, as before.
