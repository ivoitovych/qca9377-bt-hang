## 3. The disconnect reason this machine sees at power-off (new)

- [log] Archived boots, `python3 -I scripts/journal-archive-grep.py "disconnect_cb\(\) reason (21|[0-24-9])$|..." /root/bt-journal-archive/boot-*.export.zst`:
  44 `disconnect_cb() reason 21` lines, 2026-08-12 to 2026-10-11, many in the same second as
  a soft-block event (e.g. 2026-09-24 01:25:56, 2026-08-15 00:49:47, 2026-08-14 20:30:30).
  21 is not a MGMT reason (0-5): it is the raw HCI error 0x15, "Remote Device Terminated
  Connection due to Power Off".
- [source] Explained by kernel `c1cec2bbbeb5` (bluetooth tree, 2026-07-10, Mikhail Gavrilov,
  "Bluetooth: mgmt: Translate HCI reason in Device Disconnected event"; in mainline from
  v7.2-rc4 (`git tag --contains c1cec2bbbeb59` in `cache/linux`), in 7.1.y as `3899db224f8a7`
  from v7.1.6). [quoted, its message]: on power
  off "userspace sees an out of range reason ... Reason: Reserved (0x15)", and after the fix
  "a power off is reported as MGMT_DEV_DISCONN_REMOTE".
  Ubuntu `7.0.0-38` and `-42` carry it (`net/bluetooth/hci_event.c:2769` / `:2771`,
  `hci_to_mgmt_reason(cp->reason)`); `-31` and `-34` do not.
- [inferred] So on the current kernel a power-off or soft-block disconnect reaches the policy
  plugin as reason 3, the same value as a headset switched off by its user. **A narrow opt-in
  cannot key on the disconnect reason**; it has to key on bluetoothd's own knowledge that the
  adapter went down with devices connected.
- [log] Positive controls on this machine: reason 1 (link loss) gives the 7 attempts at
  1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 s (2026-08-13 11:38:06 to 11:40:44; 2026-08-18 20:39:38 to 20:42:17);
  reason 5 (suspend) gives one attempt 7 s later (2026-10-27 15:50:14 to 15:50:21, current
  journal, `journalctl _COMM=bluetoothd -g "policy.c:disconnect_cb|reconnect_timeout|configured to reconnect"`).

## 4. Upstream discussion

- [quoted] BlueZ GitHub #294 (https://github.com/bluez/bluez/issues/294, "Device
  last-connected-time API", 2022). The maintainer (Luiz Augusto von Dentz), 2022-11-15:
  "Id probably introduce a new policy rule if we really want to implement something like
  this, something like ReconnectStartup which will require the daemon to remember devices
  that were connected since last time ... we can however put a different rule like
  ReconnectStartup=all ... but I wouldn't default to it given the problems stated above."
  Then: "Id make it simpler an say ReconnectStatup=on/yes" and "ReconnectStartup=# reconnect
  up to # number of devices using LRU ordering, so in case of 1 it would behave like Android."
  His concerns earlier in the thread: other subsystems reconnecting too, privacy ("the user is
  not in control and pairing may kick in the process (e.g. half-paired cases) ... would leak
  the controller bdaddr"), and his dislike of reconnects after a daemon restart.
  [log, GitHub events API, `web/github/bluez-294-events.json`] closed 2025-09-16 by
  `github-actions[bot]` (stale), not by a maintainer. No patch was posted.
- [log] lore linux-bluetooth (full clone of epoch 0 in `cache/`, subjects; and the Atom
  search `web/lore-q-*.atom`): no patch for reconnect at adapter power-on or at startup;
  no `ReconnectStartup` anywhere. The 2013-2014 kernel "Auto connection and power on" series
  (Andre Guedes) is LE auto-connect.
- [quoted] Precedent, the suspend case (2020, Abhishek Pandit-Subedi, five versions from
  RFC to v5, landed as `ResumeDelay`/`on_resume`). Maintainer review 2020-08-04
  ("Re: [RFC Bluez PATCH 3/3] adapter: Reconnect audio on controller resume"; archive commit
  `2af01dfbfd` in `cache/lore-linux-bluetooth-0.git`, read with `git show 2af01dfbfd:m`):
  "Usually connection
  policy is handled by the policy plugin since there may be platforms that want implement
  their own connection policies ... We might need a reason code to indicate to the policy when
  a disconnect happens due to suspend logic." 2020-08-27 ("Re: [Bluez PATCH v2 3/3] policy:
  Reconnect audio on controller resume"; archive commit `37d11f86fd16`): "Id put it in
  btd_adapter_driver with a callback for suspend and resume". That is the shape that landed
  (`.resume`, master policy.c:1013).

## 5. What a narrow opt-in would look like [inferred, not drafted]

- A `[Policy]` key off by default (the maintainer's own name: `ReconnectStartup`), in the
  policy plugin, fed by a new `btd_adapter_driver` callback for power changes (his suggested
  shape for resume): at power-down, remember the devices connected with a `ReconnectUUIDs`
  service; at power-up, reconnect them once (or the N most recent).
- Not keyed on the disconnect reason (section 3).
- Whether startup (daemon start) and adapter power-on are one rule or two is the first
  question for the maintainers; #294 discussed startup only.
- Test: BlueZ's emulator can power the adapter off/on with a connected BR/EDR peer
  (mgmt-tester style); the ACL page at power-on is observable. Not attempted.
