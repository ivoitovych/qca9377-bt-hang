## What changed from v2
- **Order:** the hold fix comes first, then the refusal, then the wait (reviewer 6, recheck of
  `0824c49`). In v2 the hold fix's context held the refusal's lines, so the hold fix alone did not
  apply where only it is needed (checked: v2's patch 2 fails on 6.12.114, `Hunk #1 FAILED`). The
  final tree is unchanged: `f5424dd92043`, the tree tested in v2. Commits on `co02-send/v3`:
  `06a507520b63` (hold), `518a58be26bd` (refusal), `5a8c6dc5c641` (wait).
- **Cover letter:** the patch paragraphs follow the new order, and one paragraph tells the stable
  team which lines need which patches.
- **BlueZ test:**
  - the During case also accepts EBUSY, which kernels before 6.13 answer (an honest refusal; the
    bug, "connected at once without a link", still fails);
  - Connect Twice requires the SCO link's Disconnection Complete, with status 0, before the ACL
    link's (reviewer 3);
  - below `---`, one line offers a rebase if Luiz Augusto von Dentz's series moving the testers to
    the mainloop lands first (patchwork 14908123 rewrites `sco-tester.c`).
