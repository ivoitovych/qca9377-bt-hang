## What changed from v1 (`../patch/v1/`)
- **Patch 3 covers every reused connection.** v1 waited for a running `hci_conn_timeout()` only
  when the reused connection had a link. A SCO connection the remote device set up never gets a
  link: `hci_event.c` has no `hci_conn_link()`, and its hold comes from `__sco_chan_add()`
  (`sco.c:315`). So closing an accepted socket queues `disc_work` on an unlinked connection. A
  connect then takes the `hci_conn_link()` branch, whose hold does not wait either. v2 waits
  after both branches. A new connection has no queued work, so nothing is waited for.
- **Patch 1:** one clause on why the connect got EBUSY before `e6720779ae61`. That commit's diff
  removes the `__sco_sock_close()` path that kept the closed socket attached in `BT_DISCONN`.
- **Patch 3's message:** why the wait is safe. `hci_proto_disconn_ind()` returns a constant for
  SCO, `hci_abort_conn()` only queues the Disconnect, and `hci_conn_del()` already waits for
  `disc_work` with `hdev->lock` held.
- **BlueZ test** (`tools/sco-tester: Add SCO reconnect tests`):
  - the watch conditions are checked: `G_IO_OUT` without ERR or HUP, then `SO_ERROR` and `POLLOUT`;
  - the second link must still be up 500 ms later;
  - the ACL teardown is matched on the ACL handle from Connection Complete;
  - the ACL wait is 5 s;
  - the subject is 41 characters;
  - the diffstat is generated by `git format-patch`.
