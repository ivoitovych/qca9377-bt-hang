New. Applies on top of "[PATCH v2 0/2] Bluetooth: hci_conn: fix SCO
connection reuse" (KERNEL-V2-LINK): it adds an "else if" next to the
abort_reason check of that series' patch 2.

Tested on bluetooth-next da3cc02587c6, QEMU, 4 CPUs, KASAN and lockdep,
with the sco-tester cases of "[PATCH BlueZ] tools/sco-tester: Add
connect twice with other voice tests" (BLUEZ-P2-LINK): of the 35 cases
33 pass with patches 1 and 2 ("Connect Twice Other Voice Setting" and
"... Retry" fail), all 35 with this patch (3 runs each). mgmt-tester
(526) and iso-tester (213) pass with it (1 run each).

A link the remote device set up (a listening socket accepted it, then
its socket was closed) has no stored parameters. For a connect() that
arrives after the last holder dropped the link and before its idle
disconnect has run, held back with a debug-only change: with patches 1
and 2 it returns 0 and the socket is reported connected on the remote
device's link, with the default CVSD and with a transparent voice
setting (3 of 3 runs). With this patch it fails with -ECONNABORTED and
the next connect() gets a link of its own (30 of 30 runs that ran to
the end). While a socket holds the link the second connect() fails
with -EBUSY either way.
