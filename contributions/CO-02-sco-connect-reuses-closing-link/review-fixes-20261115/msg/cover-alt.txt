An HFP codec change can leave a headset silent. PipeWire closes its
SCO socket and connects another one to the same headset at once.
hci_connect_sco() hands the new socket the connection that is being
disconnected, so connect() reports success although no setup command
is sent, and the new socket is closed with ECONNABORTED when the old
link's Disconnection Complete arrives.

Patch 1 corrects hold ownership when a SCO connection whose link is
up is reused: the second connect() correctly returns EBUSY, but also
drops the first socket's hold and disconnects its working link.

Patch 2 refuses a connection that is being disconnected with
-ECONNABORTED, and also one whose disconnect worker is already
running, without waiting for it.

Testing: bluetooth-next da3cc02587c6, QEMU, 4 CPUs, KASAN and
lockdep, BlueZ master 20ffd285b58f with the sco-tester patches
posted separately, patches 1 and 2 applied:

  sco-tester, 35 posted cases   without the patches 31 pass; with
                         patches 1 and 2, 33 pass and 2 fail (3 runs).
                         The two that fail are "Connect Twice Other
                         Voice Setting" and its retry case: they test
                         a separate parameter-mismatch patch that is
                         not part of this series
  mgmt-tester (526), iso-tester (213): all pass with patches 1 and 2

The l2cap-tester was not run with these two patches.

The cases that pass only with the patches are "Reconnect During
Disconnect" and "Connect Twice". The race patch 2 closes was
reproduced with a debug-only change that parks hci_conn_timeout()
after it has seen no holder: with patches 1 and 2 the second
connect() fails with -ECONNABORTED instead of being reported
connected and closed later, for a link set up by the host and for one
set up by the remote device (2 of 2 cases pass; without the patches
1 of the 2 cases passes).

These patches were not run on the laptop where the bug was found.

PipeWire retries ECONNABORTED at once, while the old link is still
going down, so a codec change is still not seamless: audio returns
about five seconds later. A prototype that defers the new connect()
until the old link is gone exists; it changes connect() semantics, so
I can post it as an RFC.

For stable, patch 2 depends on patch 1 and must not be backported
without it. Patch 1 applies alone on 6.6.y and 6.12.y; patch 2 is for
trees with e6720779ae61, which is in v6.13 and later (of the stable
trees, 6.18.y and 7.3.y).

Changes in v2 (v1: https://lore.kernel.org/linux-bluetooth/20261027165009.2401154-1-yaroslav.voytovych@gmail.com/):
- v1 patches 2 and 3 are one patch, so that no commit leaves the
  race open; its second abort_reason check uses READ_ONCE() and does
  not sleep: it refuses a connection whose disconnect worker is
  running instead of waiting for it.
- Rebased on bluetooth-next da3cc02587c6.
