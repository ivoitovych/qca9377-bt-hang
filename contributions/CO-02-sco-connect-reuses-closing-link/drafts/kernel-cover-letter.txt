Subject: [PATCH 0/2] Bluetooth: hci_conn: fix SCO connect to a device whose SCO link is going down

When a SCO socket is closed and a new one connects to the same device
before the Disconnection Complete of the old link arrives,
hci_connect_sco() hands the dying connection to the new socket:
connect() succeeds without a Setup Synchronous Connection being sent,
and the socket is closed with ECONNABORTED when the event arrives.
PipeWire does exactly this when the HFP codec is changed during a call,
and with headsets that answer AT+BCS quickly the new codec never gets a
link.

Patch 1 fails such a connect with -ECONNABORTED instead.

Patch 2 fixes a related problem found while testing: a second connect()
to a device whose SCO link is up drops the hold of the socket that owns
the link, so connect() fails with EBUSY and then the first socket's
link is disconnected.

Tested on bluetooth-next <TIP> in QEMU with BlueZ's emulator, using
two new sco-tester cases ("[PATCH BlueZ] tools/sco-tester: Add
reconnect tests", sent separately): "Reconnect During Disconnect" fails
without patch 1 and passes with it, on uniprocessor and SMP kernels;
"Reconnect After Disconnect" passes on both. sco-tester, iso-tester,
l2cap-tester and mgmt-tester results are otherwise unchanged.

One question: PipeWire retries a connect that fails with ECONNABORTED
at once, which is still before the old link is gone, so with patch 1
its retries fail too. Deferring the connect until the old link has been
torn down (queueing it behind the disconnect, connect() returning
EINPROGRESS) lets that retry, or the first connect, succeed in the
same tests. Would you prefer that approach? I can send it as an RFC.

The series applies on top of Hitalo Souza's pending SCO setup series
and is independent of it.
