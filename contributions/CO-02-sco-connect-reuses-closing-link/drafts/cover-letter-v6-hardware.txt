1. Replace "The series has not yet been run on hardware; the bug was found on a QCA9377 laptop
   with PipeWire 1.0.5." with:

It was also run on the QCA9377 laptop where the bug was found (Ubuntu
24.04's 7.0.0-38 Bluetooth with abd93c85c8667, 816fb1590a4c and the
series; PipeWire 1.0.5) through a day and a half of ordinary use with
three headsets. On the earbuds that answer AT+BCS before the old link
is gone, connect() now fails with ECONNABORTED at each codec change (3
of 3) instead of succeeding without a link; on the two headsets that
answer after it, each codec change (8 of 8) still gets a new link. Every
SCO link went down at hang-up and the kernel log has no warning. Patches
1 and 3 were not reached there: nothing on that desktop connects twice
to a headset whose link is up, and patch 3's window opens only with the
debug change above.

2. Replace the paragraph "The series does not by itself make PipeWire's codec switch succeed:
   ..." with:

The series does not by itself make PipeWire's codec change seamless:
PipeWire retries a connect that fails with ECONNABORTED at once, still
before the old link is gone, so its retries fail too. On that laptop
the audio came back when WirePlumber restarted the failed stream about
5 seconds later; without the series it did not come back until the
stream was restarted. A prototype that instead defers such a connect
until the old link has been torn down lets the retry succeed in the
same tests; it builds on this series and changes connect() semantics,
so I can post it as an RFC if that approach is preferred.
