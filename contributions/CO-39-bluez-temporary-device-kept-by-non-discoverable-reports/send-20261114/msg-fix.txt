adapter: Don't keep temporary devices alive with non-discoverable reports

btd_adapter_device_found() calls device_update_last_seen() for an
existing device before it drops a report that is not discoverable.
For a temporary device that call also restarts the temporary timer, so
a peer that keeps advertising with flags 0x18 (and no active discovery
filter matching it) keeps an unpaired, unconnected device object alive
for as long as it advertises, instead of the 30 s TemporaryTimeout.

Update last seen only for reports that are not going to be dropped, or
for devices that are not temporary or are connected, so bearer selection
for paired devices still sees every report.
