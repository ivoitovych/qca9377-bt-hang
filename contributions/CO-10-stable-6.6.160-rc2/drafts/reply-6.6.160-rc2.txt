Built 6.6.160-rc2 and 6.6.159 for x86_64 with BlueZ's doc/tester.config
(KASAN, lockdep) and ran BlueZ's emulator testers in QEMU. No regressions
in Bluetooth:

- mgmt-tester (508 cases): the same verdicts on both kernels, except
  "Start Discovery - Simultaneous Inquiry Late", which fails on 6.6.159
  (Start Discovery answered Busy) and passes on rc2, 3 of 3 runs. That
  is the test for "Bluetooth: hci_event: fix simultaneous discovery
  stuck in FINDING".
- A vhci reproducer for "Bluetooth: MGMT: Fix status of pending commands
  flushed on power off" gets Command Status 0x00 for Start Discovery on
  6.6.159 and 0x0f (Not Powered) on rc2, 2 of 2 runs.
- [PENDING] l2cap-, iso-, sco-, rfcomm-, bnep-, smp-, mesh-, ioctl-,
  userchan- and 6lowpan-tester: the same verdicts on both kernels.
- No KASAN or lockdep reports.
