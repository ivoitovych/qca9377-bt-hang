Bluetooth: MGMT: Free pending mesh sends when the controller is removed

A Mesh Send is queued on hdev->mesh_pending when another transmission
is on air, and is started when that one is done. Only the transmission
whose start is queued is freed at unregister, by hci_cmd_sync_clear().
The requests behind it stay on the list: mgmt_index_removed() cancels
the delayed work that would have started them, and nothing frees them.
mgmt_cleanup() frees the requests of a socket when it is closed, but it
walks hci_dev_list, which no longer holds the controller by then. Each
request holds a reference on its mgmt socket, so the socket is pinned
too, and neither Mesh Packet Complete nor anything else is ever sent
for them. Both the requests and the sockets are reported by kmemleak
once the freed hci_dev is out of the way.

Free the pending requests in mgmt_index_removed(), after the delayed
work is cancelled and with hdev->lock held, as its caller holds it. They
are completed silently, as mgmt_cleanup() does for a closing socket;
the Index Removed event has been sent.
