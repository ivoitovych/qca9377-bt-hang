# CO-05 + CO-26 — patches and results, 2026-10-21

QEMU/KVM, 4-CPU guests (SMP kernels), BlueZ's emulator; no hardware. Raw logs, scripts,
images and `SHA256SUMS` (665 files) on the development host in `tmp/co05-w4/`; the re-check logs
in `tmp/co05-check/`.

**Base:** bluetooth-next `27731ba09195` (2026-10-20). Testers: BlueZ master `4dc15be8`
(mesh-tester 10 cases, mgmt-tester) and the project's research mesh-tester (`ab927447`, 101
cases).

## The series (`../patch/`)
1. **`Bluetooth: MGMT: Take hdev->lock in mesh_send_sync()`**: the lock after `req_lock`,
   around the capacity check, the add and the list walk; dropped before
   `hci_schedule_adv_instance_sync()` (which sends HCI commands); only the instance number
   crosses the unlock. `Fixes: b338d91703fa` (read: `mesh_send_sync()` was unlocked from the
   start).
2. **`Bluetooth: MGMT: Stop mesh_send_sync() when it cannot add the instance`** (CO-26): a failed
   `hci_add_adv_instance()` returns at once. No `Fixes:`: a client sees the same result either
   way. Separate so that 1/2 stays small for stable; folding it in is trivial.

Both: `scripts/kernel-preflight.sh` clean (checkpatch `--strict` 0/0/0, W=1 `-Werror`, sparse
nothing new in `mgmt.c`).

- **Not zero overall:** the 5 remaining KCSAN boots are readers in
  `hci_schedule_adv_instance_sync()` and its callees, deliberately untouched (a separate patch);
  the commit message says so.
- **No new lockdep report from the new lock** in 90 KCSAN boots, 3 full runs and the 12-boot
  sets. A pre-existing report (`cmd_sync_work_lock` then `unregister_lock`, found 2026-10-05)
  appears on base and patched alike.
- **CO-26, fault injected** (first mesh add fails with `-ENOMEM`, kprobe trace): before,
  `hci_schedule_adv_instance_sync(instance 6)` → `-ENOENT`, `mesh_send_start_complete(err=-2)`;
  after, no scheduling, `err=-12`. The client's Mesh Send answer and Mesh Packet Complete are
  identical.
- **mgmt-tester, 503 cases one at a time** (a whole-suite boot hung 18 min at case 109 on the
  unpatched tip): base and patched identical, 485 passed, the same 18 not passed.
- **mesh-tester:** master 8/10 on both, 3 runs; research tester 98 of 101 identical, 3
  timing-sweep cases flip on both kernels (flaky, not a difference).
