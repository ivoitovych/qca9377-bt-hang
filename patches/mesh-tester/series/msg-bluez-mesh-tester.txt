tools/mesh-tester: Add extended advertising and send sequence tests

All mesh cases run on the legacy advertising emulator (BREDRLE), so the
extended advertising path of Mesh Send, where the packet goes out through
an advertising set of its own, was not covered. Add BREDRLE50 variants of
"Mesh - Send" and "Mesh - Send cancel - 1/2": the send must enable the
mesh set (handle 4 on this emulator, le_num_of_adv_sets + 1) and the end
of a transmission is LE Remove Advertising Set for that handle.

Add transmission sequence tests on both emulators that check the handles
reported by Mesh Packet Complete in order, how often the mesh set was
started and torn down on the controller, that no Advertising Removed
event is sent for the internal mesh instance, and what Read Advertising
Features lists afterwards:

 - two sends, cancel the one on air: handle 1 completes at once, the
   queued handle 2 is then actually sent and completes;
 - two sends, cancel the queued one: handle 2 completes at once, handle 1
   completes when its count is done, nothing restarts;
 - two sends, cancel all: both complete at once, the set on air is torn
   down when its count is done;
 - two sends, no cancel: both complete in order, one after the other;
 - one send while an ordinary advertising instance is active: the
   transmission completes, advertising is not disabled as a whole and the
   ordinary instance remains.

The sequence tests get a 4 second timeout since they wait for two
transmissions and a settle time. The expectations are those of the
kernel series "Bluetooth: MGMT: remove the mesh advertising instance when
done" and "Bluetooth: MGMT: complete the mesh transmission that was on
air"; without it the new cases fail the way "Mesh - Send cancel - 1/2"
have been failing.
