doc: Enable fault injection in the tester kernel config

mesh-tester makes the first allocation of a Mesh Send's queued work
fail through /proc/self/fail-nth to check how the kernel handles a
start it could not queue. That needs CONFIG_FAULT_INJECTION,
CONFIG_FAILSLAB and CONFIG_FAULT_INJECTION_DEBUG_FS; without them the
case is aborted and reported as not run.
