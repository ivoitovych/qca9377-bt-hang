emulator/hciemu: Add hooks on client controllers

hciemu_add_hook() and hciemu_del_hook() only reach the central
controller, the one attached to vhci. A test that wants to see what a
remote receives, for example the advertising reports the emulator
delivers to a scanning client, has no way to observe the client's
controller.

Add hciemu_client_add_hook() and hciemu_client_del_hook(), the same
hooks on the btdev of a client returned by hciemu_get_client(), and
share the type mapping with the central variants.
